About Cooked

Built to make cooking feel easy

Cooked is a single-page recipe app made as a second-year JAMK TIKO software project. The goal was to build a modern, easy-to-use cooking experience where recipes are simple to save, browse, and share. Instead of a classic cookbook, Cooked gives users a fast way to add their own recipes and discover new ones with a playful swipe feature for deciding what to eat today.

What Cooked focuses on

  • Simple recipe browsing with a calm, cookbook-like feel.
  • Fast recipe creation for users who want to save their own ideas.
  • Swipe-based discovery for the “what should we eat today” question.
